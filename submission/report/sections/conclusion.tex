\section{Conclusion}

The benchmark yields four practical conclusions. First, linear TF-IDF is the strongest low-data default across all three domains. Second, learned-from-scratch neural models lead at full data, but only NSMC reaches a sustained one-point crossover. Third, the last fraction of macro-F1 carries a large training-time and model-size cost. Fourth, existing source labels make a low-label Transformer much stronger, yet standard SupCon and DB-CDSupCon add no consistent advantage over pooled CE. Since the target-only bonus crossover is unobserved, the experiment does not support a numeric target-label-savings claim.
