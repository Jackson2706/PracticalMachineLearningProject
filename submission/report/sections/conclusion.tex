\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We measured when neural sequence models trained from scratch overtake TF-IDF
classifiers on Korean review sentiment. The crossover thresholds are
\crossoverNSMC{} (NSMC), \crossoverShopping{} (Shopping) and \crossoverSteam{} (Steam).
In other words, none of the three domains shows a neural crossover within the tested range:
the best neural model never beats the best TF-IDF model by \crossoverMargin{} point, and at
$n=8{,}000$ it is still \gapNSMC, \gapShopping{} and \gapSteam{} points behind. Teams in this
regime should deploy a linear model on Kiwi-morpheme TF-IDF features and invest in more
labels. Neural models trained from scratch close the gap as data grows, but only the
extrapolation beyond 8{,}000 reviews, or a pretrained encoder, could justify their
training cost.
